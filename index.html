SenseBox Temperature Data near the Sihl river

A SenseBox carried on a 43-minute evening walk near Leimbach, in the Sihl valley (10 May 2026, 20:19–21:02 local). The sensor held a steady ~14°C and ~95% humidity, closely matching the official Zürich-Fluntern station that same evening. The stable colours are the point: the valley air was genuinely uniform, and the cleaned readings agree with ground truth.

Data & limitations

Layer 1 — SenseBox, 302 valid points (the first ~7 min / 114 points were removed as a sensor-equilibration artifact: the device read ~23°C at ~2 lux, i.e. still inside a bag). Coordinates joined from a Strava GPX track by timestamp. (sensebox GPS sensor didn't work) Layer 2 — MeteoSwiss Fluntern (SMA), 10 readings bracketing the walk window. Limitation: the dense Stadtklima Zürich network had not yet published May data and its nearest station was 1.5 km away, so Fluntern (~5 km away, 556 m) is used as the official reference. It validates plausibility rather than co-located ground truth.
